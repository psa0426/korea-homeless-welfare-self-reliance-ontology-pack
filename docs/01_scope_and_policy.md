# Scope & Policy Constraints

## Domain Boundary
This ontology covers:
- Legal/institutional concepts around homelessness support
- Service delivery functions for emergency, temporary, and self-reliance pathways
- Program outcomes and public indicators

## Out of Scope
- Clinical diagnosis details
- Individual case notes
- Internal staffing/finance operations

## Compliance Rules
- Public source citation required for each concept family
- No PII classes/attributes in seed ontology
- Evidence links must be traceable to original public URLs
